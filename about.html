Company Story

From Kenyatta Road to your doorstep

Pamba Laundry & Cleaning was founded to bring professional, reliable laundry and cleaning services to Juja — starting with a small shop on Kenyatta Road and a promise to treat every customer's belongings as carefully as our own.

Since then, we've grown into a trusted name for laundry, carpet, sofa and mattress cleaning, serving hundreds of households and offices across the area. What hasn't changed is our attention to detail — every wash, fold and delivery is handled by a team that takes pride in the work.

A shop on Kenyatta Road

To save customers time, we introduced convenient pickup and delivery across Juja.

Specialist cleaning added
